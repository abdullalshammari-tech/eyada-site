1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account information — the email address you register with, a display name, and a password hash if you use email sign-in. We never store your password in readable form.
  • Content you create or upload — topics, prompts, scripts, captions, titles, images and video files you produce with or upload to the Service.
  • Support correspondence — the contents of emails you send us, so we can answer them.

1.2 Information from platforms you connect

When you connect a social account, we receive data from that platform's API under the permissions you granted. This is described in detail in section 2 and section 3.

1.3 Information collected automatically

  • Technical and log data — IP address, browser type and version, operating system, timestamps, referring page and pages viewed. This is used for security, abuse prevention and debugging.
  • Service events — records of actions inside the Service, such as “script generated”, “asset approved”, “upload initiated”, so that we can show you an accurate history and diagnose failures.

1.4 What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your social platform passwords. Authorization happens on the platform's own domain.
  • We do not collect payment card numbers. If paid plans are introduced, payments will be handled by a PCI-compliant payment processor and we will receive only a transaction reference and billing status.
  • We do not collect special-category data (health, religion, biometrics, political opinions) and ask that you do not submit it.
  • We do not buy personal data from data brokers.

2. OAuth and connected accounts

Every platform connection uses the platform's official OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. The sequence is always the same:

  1. You click a “Connect” or “Log in with…” button inside EYADA.
  2. You are redirected to the platform's own authorization page, on the platform's domain, where you sign in and see exactly which permissions are being requested.
  3. If you approve, the platform redirects you back to our registered callback address with a short-lived authorization code.
  4. Our server exchanges that code for an access token and, where the platform provides one, a refresh token.

We store those tokens encrypted at rest and use them only to make API calls on your behalf for the features you use. We never receive, request or store your platform password. You can revoke a token at any time — see section 13.

Each platform is authorized separately. Connecting TikTok does not grant EYADA any access to your YouTube or X accounts, and disconnecting one platform does not affect the others.

3. TikTok data we access

If you connect a TikTok account, EYADA requests the permissions below. TikTok shows you these permissions on its authorization screen and you may decline any of them. We request the minimum set required for the features described, and nothing else.

ScopeData receivedWhy we need it
user.info.basic Open ID, union ID, display name, avatar URL. To show which TikTok account is connected and confirm on the post screen where a video will be sent.
user.info.profile Profile deep link, bio description, verification status. To display your account correctly in the interface and to fetch the creator information TikTok requires us to show before posting.
user.info.stats Aggregate counts for your own account: followers, following, likes, video count. To show your channel's growth over time in the analytics view.
video.list A list of your own videos with public metadata: video ID, title, cover image, duration, create time, view count, like count, comment count, share count. To measure how the content you published through EYADA actually performed, and to feed those results into future content suggestions.
video.upload No data is read with this scope. It permits EYADA to deliver a video file you approved to your TikTok account as a draft. It does not permit EYADA to publish a post. To deliver the finished video into your TikTok inbox so you can review and publish it yourself inside TikTok.

3.1 How TikTok data is handled

  • TikTok data is used only to provide the features you are using in EYADA. It is not used for advertising, profiling for third parties, or resale.
  • We do not sell TikTok data, and we do not share it with third parties except the infrastructure providers listed in section 8, which process it on our instructions.
  • We do not use TikTok data to train third-party artificial intelligence models. Your content and metrics are used to tailor your own results inside your own workspace.
  • We only access data for the account you connected. We do not access other users' private data.
  • Cached TikTok content such as cover images is refreshed or discarded in line with TikTok's requirements and expiry rules.
  • If you revoke EYADA's access from TikTok, we stop making API calls immediately and delete the stored tokens and cached TikTok data as described in section 11.

4. Your content

“Your content” means the topics, prompts, scripts, captions, images, audio and video files you create with or upload to the Service.

  • You own it. We claim no ownership of your content. See the Terms of Service.
  • We process it to run the Service — for example, sending a script to a language model to improve it, generating narration audio, rendering a video file, or transferring an approved file to a platform you selected.
  • Human review by us is limited. Our staff do not read your content routinely. Access happens only when you ask for support that requires it, when we are legally required to, or to investigate a specific security or abuse incident — and is logged.
  • Publishing is never automatic. Content is transmitted to a social platform only after you have approved that specific asset and chosen that destination.

5. How we use information

  • To create and administer your account and authenticate you.
  • To generate, render and package the content you request.
  • To display the connected account and the correct posting options before you publish.
  • To transfer an approved asset to a platform you selected, and to report back the upload status.
  • To retrieve performance metrics for your own posts and present them to you.
  • To improve the quality of results within your workspace, by learning which of your published assets performed better.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, enforce rate limits and investigate incidents.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you, and we do not sell personal data.

  • Consent — for connecting each social account and the permissions granted there. You may withdraw consent at any time by disconnecting the account.
  • Legitimate interests — security, abuse prevention, and improving the reliability of the Service, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose data by law.

10. Security

  • All traffic to the Service is encrypted in transit with TLS.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and are never exposed to the browser.
  • Passwords, where used, are stored only as salted one-way hashes.
  • Access to production systems is limited to personnel who need it, and is logged.
  • We apply the principle of least privilege to API scopes — we request only the permissions the feature requires.
  • We keep audit records of security-relevant events and review them after incidents.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and any competent authority as required by applicable law, without undue delay.

13. Data deletion and revoking access

13.1 Disconnect a single platform

Open Settings → Connected accounts in the web application and choose Disconnect. We delete the stored tokens immediately and stop all API calls for that platform. Cached data from that platform is deleted within 30 days.

13.2 Revoke from the platform itself

You can also revoke access from TikTok directly, without involving us, in the TikTok app: Profile → Menu → Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions. Equivalent controls exist in your Google Account and X settings. Revocation takes effect at the platform immediately.

13.3 Delete your entire account

Use Settings → Delete account, or email abdull.alshammari@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request” from your registered address. We verify the request, then permanently delete your account, your content, your tokens and all cached platform data within 30 days. Encrypted backups are purged on their normal rotation cycle, no later than 90 days. We will confirm in writing when deletion is complete. Deletion is irreversible.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that we have, we delete it promptly. Contact us if you believe a child has provided us data.
