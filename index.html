About EYADA

A complete operating system for intelligent content.

EYADA is an online AI platform designed to help creators manage the full content lifecycle: discovering ideas, producing content, reviewing output, publishing approved videos and analyzing results.

Built for real content operations

The platform connects content creation with measurable audience feedback. It helps organize videos, publishing decisions, platform integrations and performance data in one place.

EYADA uses authorized platform integrations only after the account owner grants permission. Users remain in control of their content and connected accounts.

Current integration scope

TikTok Login Kit is used to authorize accounts. TikTok Content Posting API is used to send approved videos from EYADA to the authorized TikTok account.

YouTube and X publishing and analytics workflows are also part of the platform's multi-channel content operations.
