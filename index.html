Product

Everything between “I need to post” and a finished, on-brand video

Most tools help you write a caption. EYADA owns the whole production line — research, script, narration, visuals, render, packaging and measurement — and hands you a finished asset to approve or reject.

Topic research

Pulls live trends, news and public creator signals, then filters them against your niche so you are not chasing topics your audience does not care about.

Script generation

Writes a short-form script with a hook, a single clear message and a payoff — in Arabic or English — following the rules your channel has been trained on.

Quality gate

An independent critic scores every draft across 11 dimensions. Anything below the bar is repaired or rejected — it never reaches your review queue.

Claim verification

Any statistic or factual claim in a script is checked against the underlying research. If a source does not support it, the line is removed — not published and corrected later.

Rendering & narration

Produces a finished vertical MP4 with narration, captions and motion — sized for TikTok and YouTube Shorts without a manual editing pass.

Human approval

Every asset lands in a review queue. A person watches it and decides. There is no setting anywhere in EYADA that publishes on your behalf.

AI Workflow

Nine stages. One of them is a person.

This is the exact order EYADA works in. The pipeline pauses at stage 6 and does not continue until a human approves the asset.

Signal intake Automated

Trends, news and public creator activity are collected and scored for relevance to your channel.

Idea shortlist Automated

Candidate topics are de-duplicated against everything you have already published, then ranked.

Script & narration Automated

A short-form script is written around one message, with a hook in the first three seconds and a payoff at the end.

Quality critic & repair Automated

Eleven scored dimensions plus claim verification. Weak scenes are rewritten; failing drafts are discarded.

Render Automated

Visuals, captions, motion and voice are composited into a finished vertical MP4.

Human review You decide

The pipeline stops here. You watch the video, read the caption, and approve, request changes, or discard it. Nothing moves forward on its own.

Platform packaging Automated

Approved assets get a title, description, hashtags and thumbnail shaped for each destination platform.

Publish You press the button

You choose the destination and confirm. On TikTok the video is delivered to your drafts, so you write the caption, pick the privacy settings and publish from inside the TikTok app itself.

Measure & learn Automated

Performance is read back from the platform and fed into the next round of decisions — so the system gets more useful the longer you run it.

TikTok integration

Log in with TikTok, upload a draft, watch the status

You connect your TikTok account once through TikTok's own authorization screen. EYADA never sees or stores your TikTok password. From then on, an approved video can be sent to your account with the metadata you choose.

  • Login Kit — authorization happens on TikTok's domain. We receive a token, never a password, and you can revoke it from your TikTok settings at any time.
  • Your account is shown before anything is sent — your nickname and avatar are loaded from TikTok and displayed on the upload screen, so you always know which account is about to receive the video.
  • Videos arrive as drafts, never as posts — EYADA sends the file to your TikTok inbox. You open the notification in TikTok, write the caption, choose the privacy and interaction settings there, and publish it yourself.
  • The caption comes with it — EYADA writes a suggested caption and hashtags and puts them one tap away, so you can paste them into TikTok instead of retyping.
  • Upload status is tracked — TikTok processes uploads asynchronously, so EYADA polls the publish status and tells you the moment the draft is ready in your TikTok inbox.
  • Your own data only — EYADA reads your profile and your own videos' public metrics to improve your next script. It does not read other people's private data.

Scopes requested: user.info.basic, user.info.profile, user.info.stats, video.list, video.upload. EYADA does not request permission to publish public posts on your behalf. What each scope is used for is listed in the Privacy Policy.

AI Learning

The part that gets better while you sleep

A generator that never sees its results just repeats itself. EYADA closes the loop: every published asset is measured, every measurement updates the model of what works on your channel, and the next script is written under those updated beliefs.

Measure

Views, retention and traffic sources are collected on a schedule for every asset you published.

Attribute

Results are tied back to the specific hook, structure and topic that produced them.

Update

Beliefs about your audience are revised — and old evidence is allowed to expire.

Re-decide

The next production run starts from the updated beliefs, not from the original assumptions.

Honest by design: when the data says a change did not help, EYADA records that too. A tactic with no measured benefit is dropped rather than kept for the sake of looking sophisticated.

FAQ

Questions people ask before connecting an account

Will EYADA post to my accounts automatically?

No. There is no auto-publish anywhere in the product, on any platform. A person has to review the asset and press publish. On TikTok, videos additionally arrive as a draft, so you get a second look inside the TikTok app before anything becomes visible.

What does EYADA do with my TikTok data?

It reads your basic profile so the post screen can show which account is connected, and your own videos' public metrics so the next script can be written with real performance data. It is never sold, never shared for advertising, and never used to train a third party's model. The full list is in the Privacy Policy.

Do you store my TikTok password?

No. Authorization happens on TikTok's own website. EYADA receives an access token, not credentials, and that token can be revoked by you at any time from TikTok's settings — or by deleting your EYADA account.

Can I disconnect a platform later?

Yes. Each platform is connected and revoked independently, from inside EYADA or from the platform's own app settings. Revoking access stops all further API calls immediately.

How do I delete my data?

Email abdull.alshammari@gmail.com from the address on your account, or use the in-app delete option. Accounts and associated platform data are deleted within 30 days. See Data deletion.

Who writes the content — the AI or me?

The system drafts it; you own it and you approve it. You can edit any script, caption or title before publishing, and you are responsible for what you choose to post — as set out in the Terms of Service.

Which languages are supported?

Arabic and English, including right-to-left captions and Arabic narration.

Is EYADA affiliated with TikTok, YouTube or X?

No. EYADA is an independent product. It uses the official public APIs of those platforms under their developer terms. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.
